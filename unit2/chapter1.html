Introduction to Collars

The collar is one of the most distinctive and versatile elements of garment design. It frames the face and neck, playing a crucial role in both the aesthetic and functional aspects of clothing. Collars can completely transform the look and feel of an outfit, making them essential to understand for fashion design and styling.

Collars are important because they:

  • Frame and define the face and neckline
  • Add structure and shape to garments
  • Provide visual balance to the upper body
  • Establish the overall style and formality level
  • Can flatter or detract from facial features and shape
  • Contribute to both function and fashion

Different Types of Collars

1. Shirt Collar (Classic/Pointed Collar)

A traditional collar with two pointed lapels that fold down and lie flat against the garment. The points extend downward on both sides.

Characteristics:
  • Two sharp, pointed ends
  • Lies flat against the chest
  • Classic and timeless design
  • Can have varying point lengths
  • Works with buttons or open neckline
✓ Best For:
  • Round or oval faces
  • Formal and professional settings
  • Both casual and dressy occasions
  • Most face shapes due to its versatility

2. Spread Collar (Wide Spread)

A collar with points that spread wide apart, creating a broader neckline opening. The points angle outward rather than downward.

Characteristics:
  • Wide angle between collar points
  • Creates a broader neckline appearance
  • Modern and contemporary look
  • Works well with ties and open necklines
  • Requires larger knot size if worn with tie
✓ Best For:
  • Long or narrow faces
  • Narrow shoulders
  • Creates width at the neckline
  • Modern, fashion-forward styling

3. Wing Collar

A formal collar with small, horizontal wing-like points that fold down. Typically used in formal evening wear and tuxedos.

Characteristics:
  • Small, horizontal points (wings)
  • Very formal appearance
  • Traditional for black-tie events
  • Always worn with bow tie
  • Creates a crisp, structured look
✓ Best For:
  • Formal events and black-tie occasions
  • Tuxedos and evening wear
  • Most face shapes in formal settings
  • Creates elegance and sophistication

4. Grandad Collar (Mandarin/Band Collar)

A simple, standing collar without lapels or points. It sits close to the neck and stands upright.

Characteristics:
  • No lapels or points
  • Stands upright around the neck
  • Simple, clean design
  • Can be buttoned or left open
  • Contemporary and casual feel
✓ Best For:
  • Long necks (creates width)
  • Square or rectangular faces
  • Casual and contemporary styles
  • Henley shirts and casual wear

5. Turtle Neck (High Mock Neck)

A high, close-fitting collar that covers most of the neck. It rolls over and extends up toward the chin.

Characteristics:
  • High-fitting neckline
  • Rolled or folded design
  • Covers majority of the neck
  • Creates a sleek, polished look
  • Can be standalone or layered
✓ Best For:
  • Long necks
  • Oblong or rectangular faces
  • Creates warmth and elegance
  • Autumn and winter styling
✗ Avoid If:
  • You have a short neck
  • You have a round face (can make it look rounder)
  • You prefer showing your neck area

6. V-Neck

A neckline that dips downward in the center, creating a 'V' shape. While technically not a collar, it's a key neckline style.

Characteristics:
  • V-shaped neckline opening
  • Can be shallow or deep
  • Elongates the torso
  • Creates a slimming effect
  • Versatile for many occasions
✓ Best For:
  • Round faces (creates length)
  • Short necks (elongates)
  • Fuller figures (slimming effect)
  • Most casual and formal occasions

7. Boat Neck (Bateau)

A wide, horizontal neckline that extends from shoulder to shoulder, following the collarbone.

Characteristics:
  • Wide, horizontal neckline
  • Extends to shoulder seams
  • Creates a streamlined look
  • Shows shoulders and collarbones
  • Classic and elegant style
✓ Best For:
  • Narrow shoulders
  • Heart-shaped faces
  • Longer necks
  • Creates width at shoulders
✗ Avoid If:
  • You have broad shoulders
  • You have a round face
  • You prefer coverage

8. Peter Pan Collar

A small, rounded collar that sits close to the neck with no points. It has a rounded, continuous edge.

Characteristics:
  • Small, rounded design
  • Sits flat against the neck
  • No pointed ends
  • Cute and playful appearance
  • Often seen in vintage or romantic styles
✓ Best For:
  • Heart-shaped or long faces
  • Creates a youthful, cute look
  • Works with vintage aesthetics
  • Shorter necks (adds interest)

9. Halter Neck

A neckline that ties around the neck from the back, leaving the shoulders and back exposed. Often used in dresses and tops.

Characteristics:
  • Ties behind the neck
  • Leaves shoulders exposed
  • Creates balance for lower body emphasis
  • Flattering for smaller busts
  • Perfect for summer wear
✓ Best For:
  • Narrow shoulders
  • Heart-shaped or diamond faces
  • Pear-shaped body types
  • Creates balance for broader hips

10. Cowl Neck

A deep, draped neckline that hangs loosely around the neck and shoulders, creating folds and drape.

Characteristics:
  • Draped, loose neckline
  • Creates soft folds
  • Elegant and sophisticated
  • Can be adjusted for depth
  • Flatters various body types
✓ Best For:
  • Long, narrow faces
  • Creates width at neckline
  • Hides neck imperfections
  • Elegant evening wear

11. Sweetheart Neckline

A neckline shaped like the top of a heart, dipping in at the center and curving upward on each side.

Characteristics:
  • Heart-shaped neckline
  • Dips at center, curves up at sides
  • Flattering to the bust
  • Romantic and feminine
  • Often strapless or with thin straps
✓ Best For:
  • Heart-shaped or square faces
  • Emphasizes bust area
  • Creates balance for pear shapes
  • Formal events and dresses

12. Off-Shoulder Neckline

A neckline that sits below the shoulders, exposing the upper shoulders and collarbones.

Characteristics:
  • Sits below shoulder line
  • Exposes shoulders and collarbones
  • Can be fitted or loose
  • Sexy and contemporary look
  • Perfect for warm weather
✓ Best For:
  • Narrow or sloped shoulders
  • Heart-shaped or diamond faces
  • Creates width at shoulders
  • Summer and casual occasions

Suitable Collars According to Face Shape

One of the most important styling rules is selecting collars that complement your face shape. The right collar can enhance your features, while the wrong one can detract from them. Here's a guide to choosing the best collars for different face shapes.

Round Face

Face Characteristics:
  • Equal width and length
  • Soft, curved features
  • Fuller cheeks
  • Rounded chin and jawline
✓ Best Collar Choices:
  • V-Neck: Elongates the face and creates length
  • Pointed Collar: Creates angles that contrast roundness
  • Spread Collar: Adds width to balance round features
  • Cowl Neck: Drapes elegantly and frames the face
  • Boat Neck: Creates horizontal lines for balance
✗ Avoid:
  • Turtle Neck (makes face look rounder)
  • Crew Neck (emphasizes roundness)
  • High Mock Neck (closes up the face)
  • Circular collars (echo the face shape)

Oval Face

Face Characteristics:
  • Longer than it is wide
  • Balanced proportions
  • Rounded chin
  • Wider forehead
✓ Best Collar Choices:
  • Shirt Collar: Works well with most styles
  • Pointed Collar: Complements balanced features
  • V-Neck: Flatters most face shapes
  • Turtle Neck: Can wear most styles
  • Boat Neck: Creates width when needed
💡 Tip:

Oval faces are versatile and can wear almost any collar style. Focus on personal preference and body proportions instead.

Square Face

Face Characteristics:
  • Equal width and length
  • Strong, angular jawline
  • Broad forehead
  • Defined chin
✓ Best Collar Choices:
  • V-Neck: Softens angular features
  • Cowl Neck: Drapes softly to soften angles
  • Round Necklines: Counteract sharp angles
  • Peter Pan Collar: Adds softness
  • Sweetheart Neckline: Curves contrast with angles
✗ Avoid:
  • Boat Neck (emphasizes width)
  • Spread Collar (adds width to jaw)
  • Pointed Collar (adds angles)
  • Turtle Neck (emphasizes jawline)

Heart-Shaped Face

Face Characteristics:
  • Wider forehead
  • Narrower jawline and chin
  • Wider at temples
  • Pointed or narrow chin
✓ Best Collar Choices:
  • Boat Neck: Widens the lower face
  • Halter Neck: Balances the face
  • Off-Shoulder: Adds width at lower face
  • Sweetheart Neckline: Complements shape
  • Peter Pan Collar: Adds softness
✗ Avoid:
  • V-Neck (emphasizes narrow chin)
  • High Mock Neck (closes the neck)
  • Turtle Neck (draws attention upward)

Long/Oblong Face

Face Characteristics:
  • Longer than it is wide
  • Narrow across cheeks
  • Long chin
  • Narrow forehead
✓ Best Collar Choices:
  • Boat Neck: Creates horizontal width
  • Turtle Neck: Adds width to neck area
  • Grandad Collar: Creates horizontal interest
  • Cowl Neck: Drapes for added width
  • Spread Collar: Widens the neckline
✗ Avoid:
  • V-Neck (elongates further)
  • Turtle Neck (too high for long face)
  • Pointed Collar (adds length)
  • High necklines (make face appear longer)

Diamond-Shaped Face

Face Characteristics:
  • Narrow forehead
  • Wide cheekbones
  • Narrow jaw and chin
  • Angular features
✓ Best Collar Choices:
  • Boat Neck: Widens the jaw area
  • Halter Neck: Balances proportions
  • Off-Shoulder: Adds width at shoulders
  • Turtle Neck: Can work with styling
  • Cowl Neck: Creates interesting drape
✗ Avoid:
  • High Mock Neck (emphasizes cheekbones)
  • Spread Collar (too wide for narrow jaw)
  • V-Neck (emphasizes narrow chin)

General Collar Selection Guidelines

Key Principles:

  • Contrast and Balance: Choose collars that contrast your face shape (round faces need angular collars)
  • Neckline Proportion: Match collar size to your face size (small faces suit small collars)
  • Occasion Appropriateness: Consider the formality and context of your outfit
  • Personal Comfort: Choose styles that make you feel confident
  • Hair and Makeup: Consider how your collar complements your hairstyle and makeup
  • Skin Tone: While collar color depends on skin tone, the shape should suit your face

Additional Considerations:

✓ For Short Necks:
  • V-neck styles
  • Off-shoulder
  • Sweetheart neckline
  • High collars to add length
✓ For Long Necks:
  • Turtle necks
  • High mock necks
  • Boat necklines
  • Collars that add width
✓ For Narrow Shoulders:
  • Boat neck (adds width)
  • Off-shoulder
  • Halter neck
  • Spread collar
✓ For Broad Shoulders:
  • V-neck (draws eye downward)
  • High necklines
  • Pointed collar
  • Avoid boat neck

Summary

Understanding collars and necklines is essential for creating flattering, well-designed garments. The right collar can enhance your facial features, balance your proportions, and elevate your overall look. Remember:

  • Different collar styles have distinct characteristics and associations
  • Your face shape should guide your collar selection
  • Collar choice affects the overall appearance and perception of your outfit
  • Personal comfort and confidence are crucial factors
  • The same collar style can look different on different people—fit and proportion matter

Use this knowledge as a guide when designing garments or selecting outfits. The most flattering collar is one that balances your unique features and makes you feel confident!
